# How do I watch or take over the agent's desktop?

Help Center › Cloud computer & workspace · https://memoh.ai/help/cloud-computer/watch-and-take-over

Open the **desktop view** in any session to watch the agent work in real time — which site it opened, what it clicked, what it ran in the terminal.

When a human touch is needed (a login challenge, double-checking a critical step), hit **Take over** to drive the cloud desktop with your own mouse and keyboard. Hand control back, and the agent continues from exactly where things stand.
