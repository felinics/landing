# How do I download and install the desktop app?

Help Center › Desktop app · https://memoh.ai/help/desktop-app/download-install

Head to the [download page](https://memoh.ai/download) and pick the build for your system:

- **macOS**: Apple Silicon or Intel (.dmg).
- **Windows**: x64 installer.
- **Linux**: .deb, AppImage or .rpm.

The page detects your device and recommends the right build automatically.
