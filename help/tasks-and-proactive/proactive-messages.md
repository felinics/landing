# Will the agent message me proactively?

Help Center › Scheduled tasks & proactive messages · https://memoh.ai/help/tasks-and-proactive/proactive-messages

Yes — it's one of Memoh's core abilities. When a release you're watching ships, a meeting is about to start, or a task fails and needs your call, the agent messages you first instead of waiting to be asked.

If it gets chatty, just tell it to reach out less or stick to one channel — and any schedule behind it can be paused at any time.
