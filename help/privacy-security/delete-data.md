# How do I delete my data or account?

Help Center › Privacy & security · https://memoh.ai/help/privacy-security/delete-data

- **Deleting a Bot** destroys its cloud computer, files and memory together.
- **Deleting your account** can be initiated under **Settings → Account**, or by contacting [support@memoh.net](mailto:support@memoh.net).

Exact retention and deletion timelines are described in the [privacy policy](https://memoh.ai/legal/privacy).
