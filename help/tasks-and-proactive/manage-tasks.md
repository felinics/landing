# How do I pause, edit or delete a scheduled task?

Help Center › Scheduled tasks & proactive messages · https://memoh.ai/help/tasks-and-proactive/manage-tasks

The fastest way is to just tell the agent:

- "Move the morning brief to 8:30"
- "Pause the repo watcher for now"
- "Delete the inbox-cleanup task"

You can also toggle, edit and delete any task by hand on the Bot's **Schedule** tab.
