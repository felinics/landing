# Do tasks run while I'm offline?

Help Center › Scheduled tasks & proactive messages · https://memoh.ai/help/tasks-and-proactive/run-while-offline

Yes. Tasks execute on the agent's cloud computer, independent of your devices — your laptop being off doesn't stop anything.

Results are delivered through the channel you choose (desktop notifications, Telegram, …), and every run's record is there in the session when you come back.
