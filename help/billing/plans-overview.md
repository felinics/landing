# What plans does Memoh offer?

Help Center › Plans, credits & billing · https://memoh.ai/help/billing/plans-overview

Memoh comes in three plans:

- **Go**: start with everyday conversations and light tasks.
- **Pro**: built for daily work and always-running agents.
- **Premium**: more compute and space for heavier workloads.

Every subscription bundles **token allowance (credits)** with **cloud-computer resources**: tiers differ in CPU cores, memory, storage and monthly credits — see the [pricing page](https://memoh.ai/#pricing) for current specs and prices.
