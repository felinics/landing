# How do I connect Memoh to Telegram?

Help Center › Messaging channels · https://memoh.ai/help/channels/connect-telegram

In your Bot's settings, open **Platforms → Telegram** and follow the linking steps. From then on you can talk to it right inside Telegram.

Reminders, quick tasks and questions all work in chat — say "remind me to pick up the package at 6" and it will ping you on time.
