**深圳猫本原理科技有限公司 (猫本公司), whose English name is Felinic**, operates the Memoh services described here. This Policy explains personal information categories, purposes, uses, recipients, storage and your rights. It is a standalone policy and does not incorporate another company's privacy policy.

“Personal information” relates to an identified or identifiable individual and excludes anonymised information. De-identified, encrypted or aggregated records remain personal information if they can still be linked to a person. **Sensitive personal information** includes information whose disclosure or misuse could harm dignity or personal or property safety, such as identity document numbers, financial accounts, voiceprints and information about children under fourteen.

## 1. Scope and processing roles

This Policy covers processing whose purposes and means we determine in our Memoh websites, applications, APIs and related tools. Each feature processes only what it needs; listing a category here does not mean it is collected by default. Optional features must explain their processing when enabled.

Where an organisation instructs us to process its users' or employees' information, it is generally the personal information handler and we act on its behalf under the relevant processing agreement. The organisation must inform individuals and handle their requests, with our legally required assistance. Our independently determined account, security and billing activities remain covered here.

This Policy does not govern independent services you use directly. It does not imply that we can access data processed solely within your self-hosted environment and never transmitted to us.

## 2. Information and purposes

### 2.1 Accounts and sign-in

We process the email address, phone number or third-party identifier required by your chosen sign-in method, together with profile name, account identifier, verification results and login records. Third-party sign-in requests only necessary information listed on the authorisation screen. Refusing necessary sign-in information may prevent account features, but should not prevent viewing public pages.

### 2.2 AI interactions and workspaces

To fulfil requests, we process prompts, conversation context, files, images, audio, video, code and tool instructions you submit, along with results, execution status and necessary usage data. Memory, file storage and history features retain content for their stated purposes when enabled. Avoid submitting unnecessary sensitive information or other people's confidential material.

Model, connector and external tool calls transmit only what is necessary for the request. **Access to local files, screen content, microphones or third-party accounts must be explained and appropriately authorised before a feature is enabled. A one-time permission does not authorise ongoing access to all device content.** This Policy does not claim that unavailable features already collect such information.

**We do not train models on your inputs, outputs or uploaded files without separate, express authorisation.** Any voluntary improvement programme will separately explain its scope, purposes, duration and withdrawal method. Refusal does not affect basic services.

### 2.3 Verification, payments and invoices

Paid services may process orders, plans, usage, payment status, transaction identifiers and refund records. Where identity verification is legally required, we will explain the basis and process necessary names, verification results and, where needed, **identity document types and numbers**. Business verification may include company names and administrator contacts. Collection depends on the specific process; payment alone does not justify indiscriminate collection of identity documents.

Invoice requests involve billing names, tax identifiers, delivery email addresses and other legally necessary tax information. Payment institutions may directly process **payment accounts and financial transaction information** under their own notices. We receive only information necessary to verify and reconcile payments and do not request banking or payment passwords in support conversations.

### 2.4 Security, operation and metering

To validate requests, measure usage, diagnose faults and prevent abuse, we process IP addresses, request times, browser or client versions, operating systems, basic device information, error logs, access records and usage statistics. Logs should avoid unnecessary complete conversations, credentials or sensitive content. Security activities require a lawful basis; “security needs” do not justify unrestricted collection.

### 2.5 Contact, support, waitlists and notices

For enquiries, complaints, infringement reports or waitlist requests, we process submitted contact details, descriptions and necessary attachments to respond, investigate or send the requested invitation. Security, billing and service-change notices relate to the service. Marketing messages require applicable consent and convenient unsubscribe options; unsubscribing does not stop necessary service notices.

### 2.6 Sensitive information and permissions

**Before processing sensitive information, we will explain the specific purpose, sufficient necessity, processing method and effects on your rights, and obtain separate consent where required. Written consent will be used when the law requires it.** Not every audio file is a voiceprint; voiceprints used to identify people receive sensitive-information protection. Refusing unnecessary sensitive processing or device permissions affects only dependent features. You may revoke permissions in device settings.

## 3. Lawful grounds

Depending on the activity, we rely on grounds provided by China's Personal Information Protection Law, including valid consent, necessity to enter into or perform a contract with you, compliance with statutory duties or obligations, and other expressly permitted circumstances. Emergency protection of life, health or property, public-interest reporting and reasonable use of lawfully public information must meet their specific statutory conditions.

Where consent is required, it must be voluntary, explicit and informed, obtained before processing. Changes to purposes, methods or categories require renewed notice and consent as applicable. Public information remains subject to reasonable scope, objections and significant-effects restrictions. “Service improvement” or “commercial interests” alone is not a general exemption from consent under Chinese law.

## 9. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request information about processing, decide on, restrict or refuse processing, access, copy, correct, supplement or delete information, withdraw consent, delete an account, seek explanations and request portability where legal conditions are met. Automated decisions are subject to applicable transparency, fairness and rights concerning significant decisions made solely by automation.

If we cannot fully comply, we explain the legal basis and appeal routes; prior publication or inconvenience alone does not justify blanket refusal. You may complain to a competent regulator or seek judicial remedies.
