# Is the cloud computer always on?

Help Center › Cloud computer & workspace · https://memoh.ai/help/cloud-computer/always-on

For your work, it's always available: schedules fire on time, long-running tasks keep moving, and channel messages always get through.

Usage allowances follow your plan. Monthly plans generally have no hard daily time cap, but sustained extremes like permanent full load fall under the fair-use policy — see [pricing](https://memoh.ai/#pricing).
